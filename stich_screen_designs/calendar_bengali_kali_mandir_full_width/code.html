Mandir Calendar 2026

A chronological guide to important dates, festivals, and observances at the Bengali Kali Mandir for the year 2026.

JAN

New year Puja

01 January 2026

Special morning aarti and community blessings for the new year.

Saraswati Puja (Vasant Panchami)

24 January 2026

Celebration of learning and arts. Special arrangements for students' hate-khori.

Republic Day

26 January 2026

Morning flag hoisting ceremony followed by patriotic cultural program.

MAR

Maha Shivaratri

15 March 2026

Night-long vigil, rudrabhishekam, and continuous chanting.

Dol Purnima (Holi)

29 March 2026

Festival of colors and special Krishna puja in the mandir courtyard.

APR

Poila Baisakh (Bengali New Year)

15 April 2026

Traditional celebrations, hal khata for businesses, and grand evening aarti.

OCT

Durga Puja 2026

16 - 21 October 2026

The grand annual festival spanning Sasthi to Dashami. Daily pushpanjali, sandhi puja, and cultural evenings.

Lakshmi Puja

26 October 2026

Evening puja dedicated to Goddess Lakshmi seeking prosperity and peace.

NOV

Kali Puja & Diwali

08 November 2026

Midnight Kali Puja, lighting of deepams, and community fireworks display.
